# Geode Project Governance

Geode is an Apache Software Foundation (ASF) [project](https://projects.apache.org/). As such, the Geode community strives to work according to the [Apache Way](https://www.apache.org/theapacheway/), and we adhere to the ASF's [code of conduct](https://www.apache.org/foundation/policies/conduct).

## Committee

Governing every ASF project is a [Project Management Committee](https://www.apache.org/dev/pmc.html) (PMC). Members of Geode's PMC oversee project development and releases. Additionally, Geode committers help maintain the codebase. See [Geode's committee roster](https://projects.apache.org/committee.html?geode) for a complete list of participants.

## Board minutes

The ASF board regularly reports on Geode project status at board meetings. Review these reports in [the meeting notes](https://whimsy.apache.org/board/minutes/Geode.html).
